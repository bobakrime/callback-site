How do I cancel my subscription?

On iPhone: Settings > your name > Subscriptions > Callback > Cancel Subscription. On Android: Google Play > your photo > Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions.

You keep access until the end of the period you have already paid for. The 14-day pass does not renew: there is nothing to cancel.

I paid and the app does not recognise it

Open the plans screen (or Settings) and tap "Restaurar compras" (Restore purchases). Check that you are using the same Apple or Google account you paid with. If it still does not appear, write to us.

How do I keep my progress if I change phones?

In Settings > "Guardar mi progreso", choose Apple, Google or your email. On the new phone, tap "Ya tengo cuenta" on the first screen and sign in the same way.

How do I delete my account and my data?

Inside the app: Settings > "Borrar mi cuenta y mis datos". Your account, conversations, reports and phrases are deleted immediately and this cannot be undone. If you have an active subscription, cancel it in the store as well.

If you cannot open the app, write to us at hhshdd504@gmail.com from your account's email address and we will do it for you.

The coach cannot hear me

Check that Callback is allowed to use the microphone (on iPhone: Settings > Callback > Microphone) and speak about a hand's width from the phone, somewhere without much noise.
