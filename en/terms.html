What this service is

This app, offered by Bobakr Imelouane Kadari, lets you practise spoken English by talking with an artificial-intelligence coach that corrects you in Spanish.

It is a practice tool. It does not replace a teacher, it is not an official language certificate and it does not guarantee that you will get a job or pass an interview.

AI-generated content

The replies, corrections, pronunciation scores and reports are generated automatically and may be inaccurate. Use them as guidance.

Plans and prices

You can do a first simulation for free. To keep practising there are three options, whose price you always see in the app before paying:

14-day pass: a one-time payment. It does not renew. When the 14 days end it simply stops being active and you are not charged again.

Monthly or annual subscription: it renews automatically at the end of each period, at the same price, until you cancel it. You can cancel whenever you want, at least 24 hours before the period ends, from your Apple account settings (Settings > your name > Subscriptions) or from Google Play; you keep access until the end of the period you have already paid for.

Payments and refunds are handled by Apple and Google under their own terms.

Your account

You can use the app without an account. If you want to keep your progress when you change phones, you can save it with your Apple account or your email (we send you a code; there are no passwords).

You can sign out or delete your account and all your data at any time from Settings. Deleting the account does not cancel an active subscription: that is cancelled in the store.
