Privacy policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

Who we are

The controller of your data is Bobakr Imelouane Kadari. You can write to us at hhshdd504@gmail.com with any privacy question.

The coach is an artificial intelligence

The coach you talk to is an artificial intelligence, not a person. Its replies, corrections and reports are generated automatically and may contain mistakes.

What data we process

Your voice: it is sent to our servers only to transcribe it (with OpenAI) and, in the repeat-a-phrase exercises, to score your pronunciation (with Microsoft Azure). We do not store the audio: it is discarded as soon as it has been processed.

The text of your conversations, the corrections, the reports and the phrases you save.

What you tell us to personalise your practice: your goal, your job title and, if you paste it, the job posting. Do not paste other people's personal data.

Usage data: minutes practised, streak, estimated level and the technical records needed to run the app and keep the cost of the service under control.

Purchase data: which plan you have, when it expires, and the purchase, renewal or refund notices we receive from the store. Payments are handled by Apple or Google; we never see your card.

Your account: you can use the app without signing up; we create an anonymous identifier and do not ask for your name or email to get started. If you choose to save your progress, we process your email or, if you sign in with Apple, the identifier and email Apple gives us, and your name if you share it. With Apple's "Hide My Email" option we receive a relay address, not yours.

An identifier of your device, only so that the free simulation is one per phone.

What we use it for and on what legal basis

To provide the service you asked for: talking with you, correcting you, generating your report and saving your progress (performance of the contract).

To measure, in aggregate, how the app is used and to fix problems (legitimate interest).

We do not use your conversations for advertising and we do not sell them.

Providers that help us

To work, the app sends data to these providers, which act on our behalf: Supabase (database and servers, in the European Union), Anthropic (generates the replies and reports), OpenAI (transcribes your voice), ElevenLabs (generates the coach's voice), Microsoft Azure (scores pronunciation), RevenueCat (manages purchases), Apple and Google (charge for purchases; Apple also identifies you if you choose to sign in with your Apple account), an email delivery service (sends you the sign-in code), PostHog (usage analytics) and Sentry (error logging).

Some of these providers process data in the United States. In those cases the transfer relies on the safeguards provided by European law, such as standard contractual clauses or the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while you use the app. If you delete your account from Settings, we delete your account (including your email and its link to your Apple account), your profile, your conversations, your reports and your phrases. We may keep purchase records for as long as the law requires.

Your rights

You can access your data, correct it, delete it, object to its processing, restrict it and ask for its portability. The fastest way to delete everything is, inside the app, Settings → Delete my account and my data ("Borrar mi cuenta y mis datos").

For any other request, write to us at hhshdd504@gmail.com. If you believe we have not handled your request properly, you can complain to the data protection authority of your country (in Spain, the AEPD).

Children

The app is intended for adults. It is not directed at children under 16.

Changes to this policy

If we change anything important, we will tell you inside the app before the change applies.
